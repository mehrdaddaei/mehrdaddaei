I hold an **M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics (Cryptography)** from **Isfahan University of Technology, Iran**.

My academic background focuses on algebraic and computational aspects of cryptography, particularly elliptic curves over finite fields. My master's thesis, entitled *“Orchard Problem in Elliptic Curves over Finite Fields,”* explored mathematical structures associated with elliptic curves and finite-field geometry.

My current research interests lie at the intersection of **mathematical cryptography, post-quantum security, and computational mathematics**. I am also exploring quantum randomness, photonic event-record analysis, entropy estimation, and statistical methods for evaluating random data.
